FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is my account safe? +
Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser using your existing session. No data is sent to any external server. We use the same connection method that Instagram's own website uses, with human-like timing patterns. Instagram's bot detection sees normal user activity.
Why is the scanning / unfollowing slow? +
By design. Scanning checks mutual status in real time (not a pre-downloaded list), which takes time but gives accurate results. Unfollowing uses randomized delays (30–60s) and batch cooldowns to mimic human behavior. This is what keeps your account safe from bot detection.
Do I need to provide my login credentials? +
No. The extension uses your existing browser session (the one you're already logged into Instagram with). We never ask for, store, or transmit your username or password. This is also why bot detection can't distinguish our requests from yours — they are yours.
What are the free plan limits? +
Free plan allows 10 unfollows per day. Scanning is unlimited. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited unfollows, select all, and customizable speed settings. Even Pro has a daily safety cap of 100–150 to protect your account.
Will the other person know I unfollowed them? +
No. Instagram does not send notifications when you unfollow someone. The other person won't know unless they manually check their followers list.
