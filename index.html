FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is my account safe? +
Yes. Everything runs locally in your browser. No data is sent to any external server. We don't store or access your login credentials.
What are the free plan limits? +
Free plan allows 10 cleanup actions per day. Scanning is unlimited and you can see all non-mutual accounts.
How do I activate Pro? +
After purchase, you'll receive a license key via email. Click "Already have a Pro key?" at the bottom of the extension and enter your key to activate.
Will the other person know I removed them? +
No. Neither Instagram nor Threads send notifications when you stop following someone. The other person won't know unless they check manually.
How do I cancel my subscription? +
You can cancel anytime from the LemonSqueezy customer portal. After cancellation, you'll keep Pro until the current period ends, then switch to the free plan.
Does it work on both Instagram and Threads? +
Yes! You can switch between Instagram and Threads with one click using the platform tabs. Each platform has separate settings and data.
