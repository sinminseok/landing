Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Following Manager for Instagram & Threads ("the Service", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information.

The Service is a Chrome browser extension that runs entirely within your browser. We are designed with a privacy-first approach — we do not operate backend servers that store your personal data.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

We want to be transparent about the data we never collect, access, or store:

  • Login credentials: We never ask for, access, or store your Instagram or Threads username or password.
  • Following/follower lists: Your follower and following data is processed entirely within your browser and is never sent to any external server.
  • Profile information: We do not collect, transmit, or store profile pictures, bios, full names, or any other personal profile data.
  • Browsing history: We do not track or record your browsing activity.
  • Analytics/tracking: We do not use any analytics services, tracking pixels, or cookies for advertising.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The Service stores minimal data in your browser's local storage (Chrome Storage API) to function properly:

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Payment Processing

Paid subscriptions are processed through our payment processor. When you purchase a Pro subscription:

  • Payment information (credit card, email) is collected and processed directly by the payment processor, not by us.
  • We do not have access to your full credit card number or other payment details.
  • The payment processor may store your information according to their own privacy policy.

4.2 License Validation

When validating a Pro license key, the extension communicates with the payment processor's API. Only the following data is transmitted:

  • The license key value
  • A randomly generated device instance ID (not personally identifiable)

No personal information, account data, or browsing data is transmitted during this process.

4.3 Instagram and Threads APIs

The Service interacts with Instagram's and Threads' APIs directly from your browser using your existing browser session. These requests:

  • Are made from your browser to Instagram/Threads servers, not through our servers.
  • Use your existing browser session cookies (we do not access or store these cookies externally).
  • Are subject to Instagram's and Threads' respective privacy policies.

5. Data Processing Location

All data processing related to scanning and following management occurs entirely within your browser on your local device. No follower/following data is transmitted to, processed by, or stored on any external server controlled by us.

6. Data Retention

  • Local data: Retained in your browser until you uninstall the extension or clear browser data.
  • Payment data: Retained by the payment processor according to their data retention policies.
  • Our servers: We do not operate data storage servers, so there is no server-side data retention.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: All locally stored data can be viewed through Chrome's developer tools (chrome.storage).
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the extension immediately removes all locally stored data. You can also clear extension data through Chrome settings.
  • Portability: Since data is stored locally in standard formats, you can access it through Chrome's extension APIs.
  • Subscription management: You can cancel your Pro subscription or deactivate your license at any time through the extension or the payment processor's customer portal.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • No external server communication for user data (eliminating server-side breach risk).
  • All sensitive operations run locally in your browser's sandboxed extension environment.
  • License validation uses HTTPS encryption.
  • The extension requests only the minimum browser permissions necessary to function.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
