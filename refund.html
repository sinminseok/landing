Refund Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all Pro subscriptions. If you're not satisfied, just email us within 14 days for a full refund — no questions asked.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to feel confident about your purchase. If you're not satisfied with the Pro plan for any reason, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase date.

  • No questions asked — simply contact us and we will process your refund.
  • This applies to both monthly and yearly subscription plans.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

2. After the 14-Day Period

After the initial 14-day period, refund requests are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. We may issue a full or prorated refund in the following situations:

  • Technical issues: The Service fails to function as described due to a bug or technical issue on our end that we are unable to resolve in a timely manner.
  • Service discontinuation: If we permanently discontinue the Service, active subscribers will receive a prorated refund for the unused portion of their subscription.
  • Duplicate charges: If you were accidentally charged more than once, we will refund the duplicate charges immediately.

3. Automatic Renewal Refunds

If you forget to cancel before an automatic renewal:

  • You may request a refund for the renewal charge within 7 days of the renewal date.
  • To avoid future charges, please cancel your subscription before the next billing date through the payment processor's customer portal or by deactivating Pro in the extension.

4. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will generally not be issued in the following cases:

  • Requests made after the applicable refund period (14 days for initial purchase, 7 days for renewals).
  • Account restrictions or actions taken by Instagram or Threads against your account. The Service includes safety features, but we cannot guarantee compatibility with platform policy changes. Please see our Terms of Service for details.
  • Violation of our Terms of Service.
  • Change of mind after the refund period, unless accompanied by a qualifying technical issue.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Send an email to simmim1768@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request".
  • Include your license key and the email address used during purchase.
  • Briefly describe the reason for your refund request (for after-14-day requests).

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are typically processed within 5-10 business days, depending on your payment provider.

6. Cancellation vs. Refund

Please note the difference between cancellation and refund:

  • Cancellation: Stops future billing. You retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period. You can cancel anytime through the payment processor's customer portal or within the extension.
  • Refund: Returns your payment. Pro access is revoked immediately upon refund approval.
